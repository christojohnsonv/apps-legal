Community Guidelines

Last updated: September 5, 2026

Happy Health Ladies Fitness provides communication features including private chats, groups, and broadcasts. These Community Guidelines are intended to help maintain a safe, respectful, and positive environment.

1. Be Respectful

Treat other users, trainers, administrators, staff, and community members with respect.

2. Harassment and Bullying

Harassment, bullying, intimidation, threats, stalking, or repeated unwanted contact is not permitted.

3. Inappropriate Content

Do not share sexually explicit, abusive, excessively violent, or otherwise inappropriate material through the App.

4. Hate and Discrimination

Content that promotes hatred, discrimination, or violence against individuals or groups is prohibited.

5. Spam

Do not use messaging or broadcast features to send spam, unsolicited promotions, scams, or repetitive unwanted content.

6. Impersonation

Do not impersonate another user, trainer, administrator, staff member, or other person.

7. Illegal or Harmful Content

Do not use the App to distribute unlawful content, facilitate illegal activity, or intentionally distribute malicious software or harmful material.

8. Privacy

Do not share another person's private or confidential information without appropriate permission.

9. Misuse of the App

Attempts to bypass access restrictions, interfere with the service, access another user's account, or exploit security vulnerabilities are prohibited.

10. Reporting Concerns

If you encounter content or behavior that violates these guidelines, please report it through the available support channels.

11. Enforcement

Violations may result in content removal, restrictions, suspension, or termination of access to the App depending on the nature and severity of the violation.

12. Updates

These guidelines may be updated when necessary to reflect changes to the App, applicable requirements, or community standards.

13. Contact

For community-related concerns, please contact us through the Support page.
